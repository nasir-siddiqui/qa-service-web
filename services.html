How We Engage

Pick the model that fits your team

Some teams need one focused cycle before a launch. Others need QA embedded in every sprint. We do both — and we'll tell you honestly which one your product needs.

Project-Based

A defined scope, a defined end date.

Best for: a pre-launch audit, a major release, or a one-off security review.
  • Fixed scope, fixed timeline, fixed price
  • Full test plan and defect report delivered
  • One retest pass on critical fixes included
MOST COMMON

Dedicated QA Team

A standing team, embedded in your sprints.

Best for: products shipping weekly or biweekly that need QA in every cycle.
  • Same engineers every sprint — real product context
  • Test cases and automation grow with your codebase
  • Direct Slack access, no ticket queue

Staff Augmentation

One or more engineers inside your team.

Best for: teams with QA processes already in place who just need more hands.
  • Engineers work inside your tools and workflow
  • Scale up or down month to month
  • No recruiting, onboarding, or bench cost
