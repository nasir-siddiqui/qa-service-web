About Verista QA

We started Verista because bugs shouldn't reach your users.

We're a team of QA engineers who got tired of watching testing get squeezed out of release cycles — so we built a practice that fits into the sprint instead of slowing it down.

Our Story

Built by engineers who were tired of finding bugs after launch

Verista QA started as a small team of manual and automation testers frustrated by the same pattern at every company we worked with: QA got looped in too late, testing got cut when deadlines slipped, and the same categories of bugs kept reaching production.

So we built a practice around the opposite approach — QA embedded from the planning stage, automated coverage that grows with the codebase, and reporting clear enough that engineers can act on it without a meeting.

Today we work as an extension of engineering teams, not an outside checkpoint, covering everything from exploratory manual testing to performance, security, API, and mobile testing.

What We Value

The principles behind every test cycle

These are the standards we hold ourselves to on every engagement, big or small.

Quality obsessed

We test the way we'd want our own product tested — thoroughly, and without cutting corners near a deadline.

Radically transparent

Clear defect reports, honest coverage numbers, and no dressing up results to make a sprint look better.

Fast by default

Critical issues get flagged within hours, not folded into a report you see the following week.

Partners, not vendors

We learn your product well enough to challenge decisions, not just execute a test script handed to us.
